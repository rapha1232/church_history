Christianity in the Twentieth and Twenty-First Centuries

8 episodes23 min

The twentieth century saw more Christians killed for their faith, and more Christians converted, than any previous century. It also saw Christianity's centre move to the Global South, and the most sustained effort at reunion since the councils. This volume covers the world wars and totalitarian regimes, Pentecostalism, the ecumenical movement, Vatican II, Orthodoxy after communism, the Middle East, contemporary controversies, and the present-day distribution of Christians. All contemporary figures are dated and sourced.
