Christianity and the Modern World (1600–1900)

7 episodes20 min

Between 1600 and 1900 Christianity met four great changes: modern science, the Enlightenment's critique of revealed religion, political revolution and the secular state, and industrialisation. At the same time it expanded across the globe, both with colonial empires and against them. Its centre of gravity began to shift south and east.
