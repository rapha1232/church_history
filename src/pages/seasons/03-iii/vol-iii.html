Season 3c. 100–313

Early Christianity and the Church Fathers

11 episodes31 min

Between the death of the apostles and Constantine, Christianity grew from scattered house-churches into an empire-wide network of communities. They were led by bishops, read a recognisable body of scriptures, defined themselves against rival interpretations, and had produced a learned literature in Greek, Latin and Syriac. They did all this without state support, and at times under state persecution. This volume traces how.
