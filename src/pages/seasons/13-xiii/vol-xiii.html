Season 137th century to the present

Eastern Christianity beyond Constantinople

7 episodes21 min

Histories written in the West often follow Eastern Christianity only as far as 1453. This volume follows its many Churches past that date. It covers the Slavs and Russia; the Churches under Islamic and Ottoman rule; Georgia, Armenia, Egypt and Ethiopia; the Maronite and other Antiochian Churches; the Eastern Catholic Churches; and the Saint Thomas Christians of India. Earlier periods for these Churches are in Volume V .
