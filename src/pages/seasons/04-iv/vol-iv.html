Season 4c. 303–451

Constantine and the Imperial Church

13 episodes45 min

In 303 the Roman state launched its most systematic attempt to suppress Christianity. Within ten years an emperor had declared himself the Christians' patron. Within eighty, a law required his subjects to follow the faith defined at Nicaea. This volume traces that transformation and the theological controversies it produced and shaped: over the divinity of the Son, the status of the Holy Spirit, and the unity of God and humanity in Christ.
