The Rise of Rome and Constantinople

6 episodes21 min

No question has divided Catholic and Orthodox Christians more persistently than the authority of the bishop of Rome. Both sides appeal to the first millennium, often to the same texts. This volume sets out what those texts say, when they were written, and how each tradition reads them. It does not assume that the structures of either the modern Catholic or the modern Orthodox Church existed in the same form in the early centuries.
