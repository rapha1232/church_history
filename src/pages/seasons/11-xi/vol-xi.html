Season 11c. 1500–1650

The Protestant Reformation

6 episodes18 min

In 1517 an Augustinian friar's protest against indulgences began a chain of events that permanently divided Western Christianity. The "Reformation" was several movements: Lutheran, Reformed, Radical and English. Each had its own theology, politics and geography. This volume presents them separately, with the main theological disputes set out at the end in their own terms.
