The East–West Schism

7 episodes24 min

The year 1054 is the date textbooks give for the division between the Catholic and Orthodox Churches. The events of that summer were real and bitter, but they were not understood at the time as the permanent separation of two Churches. This volume follows the multi-century process of separation: the excommunications of 1054, their limited scope, the Crusades and the sack of Constantinople, the failed unions of Lyon and Florence, and the modern efforts at reconciliation. It ends with a side-by-side comparison of the doctrinal issues as they stand today.
