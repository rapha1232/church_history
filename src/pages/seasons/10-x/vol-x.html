Season 10c. 1000–1500

Medieval Christianity

8 episodes26 min

Between the eleventh and fifteenth centuries the Latin Church became the most powerful institution in western Europe. It reformed itself, claimed authority over kings, founded universities, codified its law, launched crusades and repressed dissent. It also divided over who should lead it, and by 1500 faced demands for reform that it could not contain. Eastern Christianity lived through the same centuries under growing Turkish pressure. This volume follows both.
