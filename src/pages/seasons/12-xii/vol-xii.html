The Catholic Reformation and Counter-Reformation

5 episodes15 min

Historians once spoke only of a "Counter-Reformation", a reaction against Protestantism. Hubert Jedin proposed the pairing "Catholic Reform and Counter-Reformation" to capture both the reform movements that predated Luther and the deliberate response to him. More recently John O'Malley suggested "Early Modern Catholicism". This volume covers the Council of Trent, the new orders and the saints, global missions, the instruments of control, and the religious wars, and compares Trent's decrees with the Protestant confessions.
