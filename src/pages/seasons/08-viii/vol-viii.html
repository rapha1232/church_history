Season 8c. 400–1050

East and West before the Great Schism

7 episodes21 min

The break between Rome and Constantinople was not caused by one quarrel. It was the outcome of a long process in which Latin and Greek Christians came to share less: less language, less political framework, less common experience, and therefore less ability to read each other charitably. This volume traces the separate strands of that process, and then how they came together.
