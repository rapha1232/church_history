Jesus, the Apostles and the Apostolic Age

9 episodes29 min

This volume covers the life of Jesus, the emergence of belief in his resurrection, the first communities in Jerusalem and Antioch, Paul's mission, the deaths of the leading apostles, the war with Rome, and the writing and gathering of the New Testament. In this period, more than any other, the line between historical reconstruction and Christian faith must be kept visible. Historians can establish what the sources report and what probably happened. They cannot, as historians, decide claims about God's action, such as the incarnation or the resurrection, which Christians affirm by faith.
