The World before Christianity

6 episodes18 min

Christianity began as a movement within Second Temple Judaism, in a province of the Roman Empire, in a Mediterranean world whose shared language was Greek. This volume sketches that world. It does not try to be a full history of ancient Israel or of Rome. It aims to set out what a reader needs in order to understand Jesus, the first Christians and the Church that followed.

The Hebrew Bible is both a historical source and Scripture for Jews and Christians. This volume treats it as a source. It reports where historians and archaeologists find its narratives corroborated, where they do not, and where they disagree. These are historical judgements and are not meant to pass judgement on the texts' religious meaning.
