The deuterocanon in the New Testament

Ten places where New Testament writers echo Wisdom, Sirach, Tobit and Maccabees, each with the evidence from the Greek texts, from strong verbal parallels to weak ones.

An echo shows that a writer knew a book or its language. On its own it does not settle whether he counted the book as Scripture; readers on both sides of the canon question draw different conclusions. Parallels often claimed elsewhere but with no shared wording in these Greek texts are not listed.
