Saints and feasts

The Church’s year as four traditions keep it: Roman Catholic, Byzantine (Orthodox and Byzantine Catholic), Maronite and Anglican. Today’s commemorations come first, then this year’s movable feasts and the whole year month by month.

Fixed dates follow the General Roman Calendar (1969, with later additions), the Byzantine Menaion, the Maronite propers and the Church of England’s Common Worship calendar. Each calendar has many more saints than shown here. Local calendars, national days and religious orders add or move feasts. Easter is reckoned by the Gregorian rule and Pascha by the Julian rule.
