Bibliography

Chicago-style bibliography of works cited, with brief annotations. Filter by type or topic, or search. Each entry lists the chapters that cite it.

Each entry is labelled with the kind of source it is: Official church text published by a Church, council or ecumenical body; Primary source / edition a critical edition or standard translation of a primary text; University press published by a university press after academic review; Peer reviewed peer reviewed; Academic publisher specialist academic or theological publisher; General publisher; specialist author a work by a recognised specialist with a general publisher; used alongside academic works, not alone. The standards are explained under Method and sources.
