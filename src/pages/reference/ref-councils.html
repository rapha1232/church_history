Council table and encyclopedia

Which traditions recognise which councils. "Recognition" here means formal acceptance as ecumenical or authoritative. It is distinct from broader theological agreement: Lutheran, Reformed and Anglican Churches, for example, often affirm the doctrine of the first four councils without attributing infallibility to councils.
