How Christianity spread

Two thousand years on one map. Drag the year or press play to watch the faith move from Jerusalem around the Mediterranean, into Persia, India and China, north to the Slavs and Scandinavia, and from the sixteenth century to every continent.

Each point marks the first secure evidence of a church, or the conversion of a ruler, not the conversion of a whole people, which took generations. Dates marked “tradition” are not supported by contemporary evidence. Christianity also shrank: North Africa, Nubia, Central Asia and China lost most or all of their churches after the seventh to fourteenth centuries.
