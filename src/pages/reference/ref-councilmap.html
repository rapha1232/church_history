Who receives which council

E = counted as ecumenical or fully authoritative · A = accepted (its teaching received, or authoritative within that church) · P = partly or locally received · R = rejected · “·” = not applicable. Protestant churches do not count councils as infallible but generally accept the doctrinal decisions of the early councils as faithful to Scripture; Reformed and Lutheran churches differ over later ones.
