The creeds in Greek and Latin

Read the great creeds clause by clause in the language in which they were written, beside an English translation. Tap an underlined word for what it means and why it mattered.

The council of 381 used “We believe” (Πιστεύομεν); the liturgy later used the singular, as in the Latin Credo. The Latin adds Deum de Deo (“God from God”) and Filioque (“and the Son”).

The original creed of 325 ended with “And in the Holy Spirit”, followed by anathemas against Arian phrases (see the English text in the library).

The Apostles’ Creed is a Western creed and was composed in Latin; it has no Greek original.
