Comparative Theology and Ecclesiology

2 episodes10 min

This part describes the principal Christian traditions today: their origins, leadership, worship, doctrine, Scripture, sacraments or ordinances, and distribution. It then compares them on twenty-four topics in an interactive matrix. Positions are stated from each tradition's own authoritative documents where they exist. Every tradition contains internal variety, and the matrix summarises the official or majority position. Notes indicate where variation is important.
